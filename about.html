About Me

Learning to teach, one step at a time.

A quick look at my background, what I'm currently studying, and what drives my interest in education and technology.

My story so far

I completed my B.Sc. in Computer Science (Honours) at Gurucharan College, Silchar, graduating in 2024 with 74.50%. Along the way I picked up practical skills through short courses in ADCA, Advanced Excel, Power BI and MySQL.

In 2025, I began the D.El.Ed (Diploma in Elementary Education) programme at DIET Cachar, Assam — a shift from writing code to designing lessons. I'm currently in the middle of that two-year programme, learning pedagogy, child development and classroom practice while continuing to use my technical background to build small tools and resources for fellow trainees.

This website is where I bring both sides of that journey together: a technologist's approach to organising and sharing educational resources, in service of becoming a better teacher.

Learning philosophy

Learn by building

I understand concepts best when I turn them into something usable — a note set, a tool, a lesson plan.

Share what helps

If a resource helped me understand something, it's probably worth organising and sharing with other trainees.

Stay curious

Technology and teaching both reward patience, iteration and a willingness to keep learning.
